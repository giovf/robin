Acme Logistics — discovery notes, 3 November 2026

Attendees: Dana Whitfield (CTO), Sam Okafor (Finance Director).

Current state
- Order processing runs on an AS/400 that is about twenty years old.
- The web shop sends orders as nightly batch files over SFTP, so finance sees orders a day late.
- Warehouse handheld scanners talk directly to the AS/400. Nobody is sure which interface they use.
- Month-end close takes nine working days because the batch files are reconciled by hand.

History
- An event-streaming project using Kafka was started in 2024 and stalled when the lead engineer left.

Goals and constraints
- Finance wants month-end close down to three days.
- The board has approved budget for this year but wants a fixed price.
- It must be live before the summer peak, which starts in June.
- Acme has two in-house developers; neither knows Kafka.

Open points
- Who owns the scanner integration?
- Is the web shop being replaced as well, or only the order back end?
